# Day 18 – Active Listening and Reflecting Back

**Focus today:** Listen fully and then briefly reflect what you heard. Active listening shows respect. Reflecting back confirms understanding and keeps the conversation clear and connected.

Read this out loud in front of the mirror. Stand tall. Soften your shoulders. Let your pace stay steady and your face open as you speak.

---

Hello.

Today I am practising active listening and reflecting back.

Speaking well includes listening well.

When I reflect what I heard, people feel understood.

That builds trust faster than any clever sentence.

I take a deep breath in through my nose… and I release it slowly through my mouth.

I stand tall. Feet planted. Shoulders soft. Eyes on the person in the mirror.

I begin with a simple idea.

Listening is not waiting for my turn.

It is staying with what the other person is actually saying.

Then I practise a short reflection.

Someone says: “I felt unsure about the new process because the steps were not clear.”

I reflect: “So you felt unsure because the steps were not clear yet.”

I say the reflection once at normal pace.

Then I say it again, a little slower, letting the words show that I heard them.

The second version feels more attentive.

A good reflection does not add my own opinion too quickly.

It simply shows that I followed the point.

People relax when they know they have been heard.

I keep my voice warm and steady.

I do not rush the reflection.

I let it settle and then stay quiet for a moment.

If I notice I jumped ahead with my own idea, I pause and return to what they said.

No long apology. Just a clear reflection and I continue.

For example: “I hear that the timing felt tight for you… Is that right?”

The conversation stays accurate and kind.

I look into my own eyes.

I notice that reflecting back makes me look more present and respectful.

People trust speakers who can listen as carefully as they speak.

If my attention starts to wander, I soften my shoulders and lean in slightly with my posture.

The same listening can still feel warm.

I finish this practice with one full breath.

I smile at the person in the mirror.

I am learning that active listening is a skill I can practise every day.

One day at a time.

---

**Practice tip after reading:**  
Record yourself. Listen for any places where you moved on too quickly. Read those lines again, adding a short reflection of what you heard and a brief pause. Notice how the exchange feels more balanced and clearer.
