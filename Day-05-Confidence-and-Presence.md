# Day 5 – Confidence & Presence

**Focus today:** Own the space. Speak with quiet authority. Let your body and voice say “I belong here.”

Read this out loud in front of the mirror. Stand tall. Move slowly. Let silence work for you. Speak as if the room is already listening.

---

Hello.

Today I am practising confidence and presence.

Confidence is not the absence of nerves.

It is the decision to speak clearly even when the nerves are there.

I plant my feet firmly on the ground.

My shoulders drop. My chest is open. My hands rest easily at my sides.

I take a deep breath in through my nose… and I release it slowly through my mouth.

I look into my own eyes in the mirror.

I do not look away.

I do not shrink.

I take up the space that is already mine.

Many people wait to feel confident before they speak with presence.

I am practising the opposite.

I speak with presence so that confidence can follow.

My voice is steady.

I do not rush to fill every silence.

I pause after important sentences and let them settle.

When I pause, I am not lost.

I am simply giving the moment room to breathe.

I remember why this matters.

Clear, calm communication will help me in interviews.

It will help me in meetings.

It will help me introduce myself to new people as I build a life in Ireland.

I do not need to prove I am interesting.

I only need to be present and precise.

I imagine walking into a room.

I do not hurry.

I smile gently.

I greet people with the same steady voice I am using now.

If someone disagrees with me, I do not raise my volume.

I stay grounded.

I listen fully, then I respond with the same calm I started with.

Presence is not performance.

It is the quiet choice to stay in my own body and speak from there.

I finish this practice standing a little taller than when I began.

I smile at the person in the mirror.

I am learning that confidence grows every time I show up and speak with intention.

One day at a time.

---

**Practice tip after reading:**  
Record yourself. Watch the video as well as listen. Notice your posture and whether you maintained eye contact with the camera (or mirror). Practise the opening and closing again while deliberately standing taller and pausing longer after each key sentence. Feel how presence changes the way the same words land.
