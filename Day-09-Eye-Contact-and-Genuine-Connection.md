# Day 9 – Eye Contact & Genuine Connection

**Focus today:** Meet the eyes of the person in front of you — even when that person is you in the mirror. Let connection feel calm and natural.

Read this out loud in front of the mirror. Stand tall. Soften your face. Look into your own eyes more often than you look away. Smile when it feels true.

---

Hello.

Today I am practising eye contact and genuine connection.

Words carry the message.

Eyes carry the feeling behind the message.

I take a deep breath in through my nose… and I release it slowly through my mouth.

I stand tall. Feet planted. Shoulders soft. Face relaxed.

I look into my own eyes in the mirror.

I do not stare hard.

I simply stay there for a few seconds.

It feels different at first.

That is normal.

Most of us look away quickly because we are not used to being seen.

Today I practise staying a little longer.

When I speak, I keep the connection.

I say a sentence, then I let my eyes rest on the person in the mirror.

I do not look at the floor between thoughts.

I do not check my phone in my mind.

I stay present.

Genuine connection does not mean intense staring.

It means I am here with you.

In conversation I can look at the person’s eyes when I am listening.

When I speak, I can look at them for most of the sentence, then glance away briefly if I need to think, then return.

The return matters.

It says: I have not left the conversation.

I practise a short line while holding eye contact.

“I am glad to be here.”

I say it once while looking at my own eyes.

Then I smile softly and say it again.

The smile reaches my eyes too.

People notice the difference.

Warmth travels faster through the eyes than through words alone.

In interviews this helps me.

I will look at the interviewer when I listen to the question.

I will look at them when I give the answer.

If I need a moment to think, I can glance aside for one second, then come back.

I do not need to perform confidence.

I only need to stay connected.

If eye contact feels uncomfortable, I soften my gaze.

I look at the whole face, not just the eyes.

I breathe and keep speaking.

The discomfort lessens with practice.

I look into my own eyes again.

I notice that the longer I stay, the calmer I feel.

I am not judging myself.

I am simply present.

Connection begins with the willingness to be seen.

I finish this practice with one full breath.

I smile at the person in the mirror.

I am learning that eye contact is not a performance.

It is a quiet invitation to connect.

One day at a time.

---

**Practice tip after reading:**  
Record yourself on video. Watch the playback with the sound off first. Notice how often your eyes leave the camera or the mirror. Then watch with sound and practise the same two sentences again, deliberately holding eye contact for the full length of each sentence. Feel how much more present and warm the same words become.
